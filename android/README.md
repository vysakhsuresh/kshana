# Kshana on Android

A thin Kotlin/Compose shell over the Rust engine. The engine is compiled per ABI with
`cargo-ndk`; the Kotlin bindings are generated by UniFFI from `crates/kshana-ffi`. No
astrology or astronomy is written in Kotlin.

The app opens on the **Loop**: what this moment holds in plain words, a countdown to
the next shift (hour, star, tithi, Moon sign, dasha level, Rahu kala, sunrise…), nine
lives scored 0–100 (Love, Career, Money, Health, Family, Study, Home, Travel,
Children), the day's twenty-four planetary hours, the windows to watch, and what is
coming. Tapping a life opens its page: now, today's best hours, this week, this month,
this year, each with a trend line, the best and lowest stretch, and what changes when.
The loop recomputes at every shift; every number can be opened to its reasons and rule
ids (`docs/RULES.md`, Forecast section). A travel row steps the whole loop forward or
back (−1d, +1h, +1d, +7d, +30d) and back to now. The five doors are Loop, Ask, Plan,
Us and More; Remedy is a button on the Loop, on every life page and under More.

**Ask** takes a life and one of six questions (will it happen, when, is now a good
time, should I, will it come back, will it be set right) and answers from the birth
chart, the running periods, the next 180 days and the horary chart of the instant:
a verdict, a confidence, the window to act in, the first opening by the ruling
planets, and the reasons. No typing, no network, no language model.

**Remedy** locates the cause (the weakest running lord or the lord of the matter's
house) and ranks its dictionary: fourteen to fifteen remedies per graha plus six for the
life asked about, with the kind, day, effort and source of each, and commitments the
person can hold.

Every life page opens to **Why now · who · when it eases**: each graha behind the
number with its signed pull and the exact instant it moves on, every coming change
with its solved gain in points, and the three-year slow line with its turning points.

**Plan** reads a scheduled time for a purpose (meeting, interview, exam, journey, deal,
doctor, date, family, property, a start, anything): a verdict, the reasons, the better
hours that day and a better day in the fortnight.

**Us** puts another person's birth beside yours: the thirty-six-point match with its
eight kootas, Kuja dosha, how the two charts stand this second, twelve months back and
twenty-four ahead, what lifts each side next, and any of the six questions answered
from both charts together.

**The book of your life** (from the Loop or More) lays out this year's chapter (solar
return to solar return, with its rising sign, lord and a tone per life), the chapters
and pages (Vimshottari), every sade sati and dhaiya from birth to the second, Jupiter's
passages, and the Saturn, Jupiter and Rahu returns, with the present marked.

The five doors are **Now** (the Loop), **Ahead** (your day, week, month and year in
plain words, each life with the day to use, the day to go easy and what to do, the
astrology behind each line under one tap), **Ask** (a question, or a time you have),
**Us** and **More**. The Loop names the day and the moment, draws the real
sky behind the hero (the signs and the planets at their true places, the ascendant to
the left), traces the day's hours round the score ring, names **your next good
minute** for the weakest main life, dims a shade inside Rahu kala, Yamaganda and
Gulika, taps softly when the hour turns, and shows **the wall** (what you are waiting
for, counting down), **your people today** (each person's reading and the talk window
for the two of you), and bells on every shift so the phone tells you when it comes.
**The wall**, **the month's letter**, **what the sky said on…** (hindsight on any date)
and **did it happen?** (the proof feed, also asked at sunset) are a tap away. Plan
compares readings side by side, by place, and finds the week's best hours for a
purpose. Us holds everyone added with their relation, matches any two of them, and
names the talk window. Remedies now include food for the graha's people, its creatures
to feed, its water and trees, and its colours, with a daily tick and a streak on every
held remedy, and the hour's mantra can be chanted aloud by the phone, counted to 108.

The Loop also carries **the reading** (the birth Moon stated first, the periods with
their ends, Saturn's phase and exit, the moment in words, days to circle, the remedy
in brief), **the hour's lore** (deity, colour, direction, metal, mantra), a **name for
the moment**, a **date picker** on the travel row, and a **widget** and **sunrise
notification** (Settings) that carry the reading to the home screen and the morning.
Ask holds a **dictionary of forty-nine matters** under the nine lives. Under More:
**Find the true minute** (rectification from dated events) and **Your record** (every
window judged afterwards, scored by the engine). The app's own words are available in
**English, Malayalam and Hindi** (Settings → Language); the engine's sentences are
English for now.

More holds the expert screens: Birth (profile, place, uncertainty), the sky now (live
clock, wheel, panchanga), Chart (Western wheel, aspects, stability under the
birth-time window), Vedic (North Indian chart, vargas D1–D60, Vimshottari, panchanga
with sunrise, hora and Rahu kala), Moments (exact instants), Reading (prediction with
rule trace, both traditions, feedback), Areas (promise and timing funnel), Muhurta,
Settings (ayanamsa, house system, provenance) and Trust.

The sources type-check with `scripts/kotlin-check.sh` (Maven artifacts only, no SDK) and
the debug APK builds and runs on a device (moto g84, Android 15) with the steps below.
The code targets AGP 8.7, Kotlin 2.1, Compose BOM 2024.12; Gradle 8.11 needs a JDK 17–21
(the JBR bundled with current Android Studio is Java 25 and is rejected).

## One-time setup

```sh
rustup target add aarch64-linux-android armv7-linux-androideabi x86_64-linux-android
cargo install cargo-ndk
export ANDROID_NDK_HOME=~/Android/Sdk/ndk/<version>
./scripts/fetch-ephemeris.sh pypi            # data/de421.bsp + data/finals2000A.all
```

## Build the engine, bindings and kernel assets

```sh
./android/build-engine.sh
```

This produces `android/app/src/main/jniLibs/<abi>/libkshana_ffi.so`, the generated
`android/app/src/main/java/uniffi/kshana_ffi/kshana_ffi.kt`, and copies the kernel and
IERS file into `android/app/src/main/assets/` (all three are gitignored build outputs).

Then build the APK with Android Studio or `./gradlew :app:assembleDebug` from `android/`.

## How the app talks to the engine

```kotlin
val engine = Engine.open(spkPath, eopPath)        // once per process, see EngineProvider
val chart  = engine.chart(Instants.now(), 9.9312, 76.2673, 0.0,
                          HouseSystem.PLACIDUS, AyanamsaChoice.LAHIRI, false)
val next   = engine.nextEvents(Instants.now(), 14u, AyanamsaChoice.LAHIRI)
val chain  = dashaChain(chart.panchanga.moonSidereal, birth, Instants.now(), 5u)
```

Every record carries the provenance strings the UI shows, so the user can always see
which kernel, which UT1 source and which models produced a number.

## Ephemeris data

The app ships `de421.bsp` (17 MB, 1900–2050) inside the APK's assets and opens it from
the files directory. DE440s (32 MB, 1849–2150) replaces it when fetched from NAIF.
Nothing is computed on a server.

## The look: a night sky

Every screen sits on one living sky (`ui/theme/Mystic.kt`): a seeded starfield that
twinkles slowly, a faint tint at the top that follows the hour (rose at dawn, indigo by
day, amber at dusk, deep night after) and a soft gold glow in one corner. Screens are
transparent over it. Cards are glass (`Modifier.glass`): translucent, with a hairline
that catches gold at the top. Section titles carry a small diamond and a gold hairline.
Score rings draw themselves to their number, carry a halo of their own colour and a
bright head on the arc. Countdowns to the next good minute breathe. While the engine
works, a wheel of twenty-seven points (the nakshatras) turns with one lit, never a
spinner. Screens fade into each other. The one gold button keeps its warmth with a
gradient and a halo; everything else is outlined in gold hairline.

## Plain words only

The common screens (Now, Ahead, Ask, Us, the life pages, remedies, the wall, the letter,
look back, the book, notifications and the widget) show predictions in plain English
only: what happens, when, how strongly, what to do. Engine keys such as a period's lord
or an hour's planet are turned into words by `ui/screens/Plain.kt` and never printed.
The chart pages (wheels, Vedic chart, KP areas, muhurta, horary, the technical settings
and the trust report) are hidden until Settings › "Show the chart pages" is switched on
(`data/Expert.kt`, off by default).

## The new territory (1.0.0)

- **Welcome and past check.** A fresh install opens on a three-line welcome with
  "restore from my backup file". After a first birth, the app reads 3–5 real stretches
  of the last years back as yes/no questions before predicting anything
  (`PastCheckScreen`, engine `past_checks`). Every yes goes into the journal.
- **Your data is never lost.** `data/Backup.kt` writes everything the person entered to
  one JSON file: on demand (Settings › Your data) and automatically to
  Downloads/Kshana/kshana-backup.json on every stop (Android 10+). Restore from the
  welcome screen or Settings.
- **Proof in the open.** The Now screen carries "How right has it been for you?" from
  the graded record; the journal (`JournalScreen`) sets each logged day beside what the
  app read for it, marks matches and misses, and feeds "Find your exact birth time".
- **Remedies as a tree.** `RemedyTreeScreen` (engine `remedy_tree`): each pressure on a
  life, what it does, until when, how strongly; under it the help in branches: deeds
  anyone can do, giving, habits, nature, quieting the mind, prayer in one's own faith,
  and the Hindu tradition (optional). Settings › Remedies for you chooses which faith
  branches show (`data/Faith.kt`). Any remedy can be taken on for 21 days with a daily
  tick (`data/RemedyPlan.kt`); giving remedies open the person's UPI app to the cause
  they chose, or a food charity. The sunrise reading and widget name today's practice.
- **Ask in your own words.** Typed or spoken (English, Malayalam, Hindi) and mapped on
  the phone to a life, a matter and a kind of question, shown back as "Understood as…".
  Answers can be read aloud (`system/Speak.kt`) and say how sure they are in words.
- **Care.** Returning three times in a week to a painful question about love, health or
  family, or a very low health reading, shows a gentle card with Tele-MANAS (14416).
- **Calendar check.** `CalendarScreen` reads the next seven days of appointments (only
  when allowed, never stored) and marks the ones in weak hours with a better hour.
- **People.** Share a person as a kshana://person link or QR code; paste or open one to
  add them. "A date for all of us" (engine `best_slots_together`) finds hours that suit
  several people for a wedding, a house-warming or a trip.
- **Share cards.** Any day, outlook or answer becomes a picture for WhatsApp
  (`system/ShareCard.kt`).
- **Languages.** The outlook, the moment, the season, the remedy tree and the past check
  are written in Malayalam and Hindi by the engine itself; the rest falls back to English.
- **How Kshana works** (More): what it reads, how a reading is made, how sure it is, what
  it cannot do, remedies, data, and the privacy policy.
- **Play Store ready.** Signed release bundle, 16 KB-aligned native libraries, R8 rules,
  crash log kept on the phone (Settings › Problem report), docs/PLAY_STORE.md and
  docs/PRIVACY.md.

## Five doors (1.1)

- **NOW**: today. On your mind (each worry, today's standing and the date the reading
  gives), the record, the lives you chose (the rest in one ALSO line), the wall, people,
  hours, the clock. Ask opens from here.
- **READING** (`ReadingScreen2.kt`, `ConsultScreen`): a sitting with an astrologer on one
  page. What is on your mind, each answered with a verdict, how sure, the window of
  dates and three doors (why, what helps, ask more); your past read back as plain
  statements with "that happened" (engine `past_checks` statement); where you stand; what
  is coming this year (top five from `future`); what will help (deeds first); how sure
  all of it is. Every section ends with a door (`Door` in Common.kt) to the full screen.
- **FUTURE** (`FutureHub`): DAY TO YEAR (the Ahead screen) and YEARS AHEAD (the timeline).
- **US** and **MORE** as before.

Situations (`data/Situations.kt`) are the 37 worries people most often bring to an
astrologer, grouped as Marriage and love, Work, Money, Going abroad, Health and mind,
Home and family, Studies, Children, and one fear: "an astrologer said something is wrong
in my chart", answered with what is actually there, when it ends, and a plain warning
against paying large sums to remove a "dosha". Each maps to a life, a matter (49 plus a
new "court case") and a kind of question. The person's choices are kept on the phone and
in the backup.
