# Rule catalogue

Every sentence Kshana shows a user traces to one of these rule ids. The id is stable;
the source names the text the rule is taken from. "Convention" marks a point where
schools differ and states the choice made.

## Ask

A question about one life is always answered, and each question is its own
computation. The common ground is four sources, each in [0, 1]:

| Source | What |
|---|---|
| natal | KS-N-0001, the birth chart's promise for the life |
| period | 0.6 × (agreeing Vimshottari levels ÷ 5) + 0.4 × the life score now |
| ahead | the next 180 daily samples: their mean and their best stretch |
| witness | the KP horary verdict for the instant and place of asking (KS-K-0001, KS-P-0012): Yes 1.0, Delayed 0.6, Unclear 0.5, No 0.2 |

| Question | How it is answered |
|---|---|
| Will it happen? | natal 0.35 + period 0.30 + ahead 0.20 + witness 0.15; Yes ≥ 0.62, Yes with patience ≥ 0.50, Not yet ≥ 0.40, else Not in this period; the window is the nearest run of two days at or above the verdict's floor |
| When? | the soonest of (a) the first run of three days at or above good in the 180-day series and (b) the first antardasha or pratyantar beginning within two years whose lord signifies the area's houses; the verdict names the horizon (within the month, three months, six, the year, beyond); the score falls with distance (0.85, 0.7, 0.55, 0.42, 0.32); the 24-month slow line's first climb of a band is named; when nothing opens, "Not within two years" |
| Is now a good time? | a caution window running caps it at "Not this minute"; the life score ≥ 0.56 is "Go ahead"; else the best remaining hour by its fit; else "Not today" with the next good stretch |
| Should I? | the next seven days' mean against the best stretch of the 180 days, on the natal ground: "Take it now" when the week is within 0.06 of the best and the chart carries it; "Take it, from the window" when a clearly better stretch exists; "Hold" when the ground is below 0.45 and the week below 0.5; else "Take it, with small stakes" |
| Will it come back? | the slow line twelve months back and twenty-four ahead: a dip of 0.05 below the past year's peak that climbs back within 0.03 is "Yes, it comes back" around that month; a dip that does not is "Not as it was"; no dip is "Nothing has left the chart" |
| Will it be set right? | Explain's first lasting lift and the slow line's first climb of a band: "Yes, it mends" from that month; a lift without a climb is "It steadies, slowly"; neither is "Not in this period" |

Confidence is 1 − 2.5 × the standard deviation of the sources each question weighs,
clamped to [0.3, 0.95]. The first opening is the first Moon window of the horary
chart's ruling planets within 30 days (KS-P-0020). The Moon sub-lord's "genuineness"
(KS-P-0001) is reported as a reason, never used to refuse an answer. Under Us each
chart answers in its own terms and the pair's verdict says whether the two agree and
on whose side the wait is.


## Explain (why now, who, when it eases)

Every component of a life score is opened to its grahas. Each running period lord, each
transiting graha, the Moon's star, the planetary hour and the limbs of the day becomes a
*cause* with a signed pull (its weight × (score − ½), the transits split by graha as
relevance × value × ashtakavarga factor ÷ Σrelevance) and the exact instant it ends: the
period boundary, the graha's next sidereal sign change (solved to the second on the
apparent longitude and the ayanamsa of that instant; the osculating node for Rahu and
Ketu), the next nakshatra boundary, the hour's end, the window's end. For every cause
that ends within two years the component is recomputed with that one graha moved on
(the new five-level chain after a period ends; the new house from the Moon after an
ingress; the next tara; the next hora lord) and the difference is reported as the
*relief*, in points. A monthly *slow* score (birth chart, periods, transits of the eight
grahas other than the Moon, renormalised) over thirty-six months gives the turning
points: the first month the life climbs a band, the peak, the first month it slips.

## Plan (how is this time for me)

A purpose names the lives it touches and their weights (meeting: career 0.30, money
0.20, love 0.20, family 0.15, travel 0.15; interview: career 0.70, money 0.20, study
0.10; exam: study 0.70, career 0.20, health 0.10; journey: travel 0.60, health 0.20,
money 0.10, family 0.10; deal: money 0.50, career 0.30, home 0.20; doctor: health
0.70, family 0.15, money 0.15; date: love 0.70, family 0.15, travel 0.15; family
matter: family 0.60, love 0.20, children 0.20; property: home 0.60, money 0.30, family
0.10; starting something: career 0.30, money 0.30, study, travel, home, health 0.10
each; anything: all nine equally). The reading is the weighted mean of the lives'
scores at the instant (the full forecast, KS-N through KS-M). Inside Rahu kala,
Yamaganda or Gulika it is capped at 0.40 and the verdict is "Shift it if you can";
inside Abhijit it gains 0.05 (Muhurta Chintamani). Verdicts: Go ≥ 0.62, Go with care
≥ 0.50, Shift it ≥ 0.40, else Avoid. Better hours the same day are the planetary hours
outside the cautions whose weighted hora fit beats the chosen hour's by 0.08; a better
day is the best of the next fourteen daily samples when it beats the chosen time by 0.08.

## The briefing

What an astrologer would say across the table, assembled from the rules above and
nothing else: the birth Moon's sign and star (stated first, so any other reading can be
checked against it: a different Moon sign means a birth time off by hours); the
mahadasha and antardasha with their end instants; Saturn's phase relative to the Moon
with its exit, found by walking Saturn's sidereal sign changes ahead and treating a
retrograde step out and back within 1.5 years as a pause, so the date agrees with the
Book; the moment (overall band, strongest and weakest life with their reasons, the
hour, the Moon's star and tara); the days to circle (the best of the next thirty daily
samples for each chosen life); the remedy in brief (the parihara plan for the weakest
chosen life when it is below good, its first three non-optional entries); and, when
that life is steady or lower, its heaviest lasting cause and first lasting lift from
Explain. Under Us the second chart's sentences are turned to the third person, and the
pair gets its own paragraphs: the two seasons, the Moon-to-Moon angle, which side the
wait is on, and the first lasting lift for either. The match's doshas (Nadi, Bhakoot,
Gana, unbalanced Kuja, low Graha Maitri, low Yoni) each carry their classical remedies
(Dharma Sindhu and Nirnaya Sindhu dosha parihara, the Vivaha tradition, Lal Kitab) with
ritual items marked optional.

## The hour's lore and the moment's name

Each hora lord carries its deity, colour, direction, metal, beeja mantra, and what the
hour is for and what to keep from (Navagraha beeja mantras of the Agama tradition, the
Navagraha dik and dhatu tables, Hora shastra). A moment is named from its tara, its
hour and the Moon's phase: "Sampat under the Moon's hour, waxing".

## The year's chapter

The solar return is the exact instant the Sun stands again at its natal tropical
longitude, solved on the kernel; the year runs return to return. The return chart is
cast where the person is; its rising sign names the year and its lord is the year's
lord (convention: the simplest Tajika choice of varshesha). Twelve monthly slow samples
from the return give each life its tone for the year, and the best and the weakest.

## Rectification

Every candidate birth time inside the stated window (at most 241, evenly spaced) is
cast. For each dated event the candidate's slow score for the event's life on that day
and the Vimshottari funnel's agreement (levels carrying the life's houses, out of
three) are combined: a good event scores 0.6 × slow + 0.4 × agreement, a hard one the
complement. The fit is the mean over the events; the best candidate of each rising
sign the window crosses is shown, since the rising sign and the Moon's star are what
the window can change. The fit ranks the window against the person's own history; it is
not a proof, and two times that explain everything equally are both kept.

## The person's record

Every window the app named on a life page can be judged after it passes. A window at
or above good that happened is a hit, and so is one below good that did not. Once a
life has five answers, half the mean gap between what happened (1 or 0) and what was
predicted becomes an offset bounded to ±8 points, shown beside the score as "by your
record". The core score is never altered silently.

## The outlook in plain words

Today, this week, this month and this year are written for the person, never for the
planet. Today: each life's band sentence and its best remaining hour (KS-H-0001 ≥ 0.56,
outside the cautions). Week and month: the daily series' story per life (mean, trend,
best and lowest stretch) in a sentence with dates. Year: thirteen monthly slow samples
per life (mean, trend, best and lowest month), and for the two weakest lives the first
climb of a band from Explain. Each life carries one line of advice by band and the
astrology that produced it as `why`, shown only on a tap. The season line names the
nearer period's end and Saturn's phase in plain words (sade sati "the long
seven-and-a-half-year test"; the 4th "a testing stretch for home and peace of mind";
the 8th "a testing stretch for health and sudden turns"), with the Sanskrit in brackets.

## The feed, the wall and the observances

The next good minute for a life is the first planetary hour, outside the cautions, at
or above 0.60 for it (KS-H-0001). The wall is every instant the person waits for:
Saturn's exit or next move, the next sade sati (Saturn entering the 12th from the
Moon, scanned ahead to the kernel's end), the ends of the running mahadasha, antardasha
and pratyantar, Jupiter's and Rahu's next sign with its house from the Moon, the next
solar return, the Moon's return to the birth star, the next Pournami and Amavasya,
and the next Jupiter and Saturn returns, each solved to the second. The observances
are the tithis of the month (Shukla Shashthi 6, Durga Ashtami 8, Ekadashi 11 and 26,
Pradosham 13 and 28, Pournami 15, Sankashti Chaturthi 19, masik Shivaratri 29,
Amavasya 30), the Sun's sankranti, and the Moon's entry into the birth star. The
day's name is its tara, its tithi and the weekday's lord ("Sampat Shashthi, the
Moon's day").

## The month's letter

Thirty daily samples from the day it is written: each life's story (trend, best and
lowest stretch), the days to circle (the briefing's), the observances, the nearest
countdown on the wall, and the remedy in brief for the weakest life, set out as
paragraphs with dates.

## The best slots and the talk window

The best slots for a purpose in the days ahead score every planetary hour outside the
cautions half by its weighted hora fit and half by the day's weighted reading from
the daily series. The talk window for a pair is the first hour in the days ahead when
both charts' hora fits for love are at or above 0.55, outside the cautions, on a day
when both daily love scores are at or above 0.50.

## Food, creatures, water and colour

The remedy dictionary carries, beside the karma, seva, discipline, daana, vrata,
mantra and pooja of each graha, its food given to its people (anna), its creatures
fed (jeeva: the crow for Saturn and the ancestors, the cow for Jupiter and Venus, the
dog for Ketu, ants and fish and birds for Rahu, monkeys for Mars), its water and trees
(prakriti: the peepal for Saturn and Jupiter, the tulasi for Venus and Mercury) and
its colours worn and kept from (vastra), from the Puranic daana lists, Lal Kitab and
temple practice. Rahu's remedies are feeding those outside the circle: the outcast,
the stranger, the one sleeping on the street, and the small and many creatures.

## The book of a life

Chapters are the mahadashas of the 120-year Vimshottari cycle, pages the antardashas of
the running one; each has a tone per life from the lord's KP signification and
Parashari period quality (a page blends its own lord and the chapter's, half each).
Saturn's trials are every sade sati (Saturn in 12, 1, 2 from the Moon) and dhaiya (4,
8) from birth to age 100 or the kernel's end, from every sidereal sign change of Saturn
solved to the second; a retrograde loop that leaves and re-enters within 1.5 years is one
trial. Jupiter's passages are its sign changes three years back to twelve ahead, each
house marked favourable by the transit table (2, 5, 7, 9, 11). Returns are the instants
Saturn, Jupiter and Rahu stand again at their natal sidereal longitude, from two years
after birth, with crossings under 1.5 years apart counted once.

## Parihara

Remedies are keyed to the graha located as the cause (the weakest running period lord,
or the lord of the area's house). Each graha carries a dictionary of fourteen to
fifteen entries across seven kinds (karma, seva, discipline, daana, vrata, mantra,
pooja), each with its day, effort and source: the dharma reading of the graha's
signification, BPHS Graha shanti (japa counts and deities), the Puranic graha-daana
lists, Lal Kitab, and the Navagraha kshetra tradition. When the life is known, six
more entries for that matter are added (the marriage vratas, Lakshmi for wealth,
Mahamrityunjaya for health, Saraswati for study, and so on). Karma, seva and discipline
rank above daana and vrata; mantra and pooja are marked optional. Conduct items follow the dharma-shastra reading
of each graha's signification; ritual items follow common temple and Lal Kitab practice.

## Plain words (the person never reads the astrology)

The engine computes with grahas, houses, periods, stars and transits; the person reads
only what they mean for their life. Every user-facing sentence is built from
`kshana-rules/src/plain.rs`:

| Computed | Read by the person as |
|---|---|
| A major period's lord | `chapter_theme` / `chapter_prediction`: what the years are about and what they tend to bring |
| A graha pressing on a life | `pressure`: e.g. "delays, heavy duty and tiredness" |
| A graha helping a life | `support`: e.g. "luck, wise advice and growth" |
| The planetary hour | `hour_nature`: what the hour is good for |
| The tara of the day | `day_quality`: "a day that gives", "a day to hold back" |
| Saturn from the birth Moon | `long_test` / `long_test_prediction`: "a testing time for home and peace of mind" and what it brings |

`plain::jargon` is the checker (planets in English and Indian names, houses, lagna,
periods, transits, stars, taras, signs, sade sati, dosha, koota and the rest). The FFI
test `plain_audit` calls every engine function the app shows to a person, for three
people at three instants, and fails on any such word in a field the person reads. Keys
and the expert layer (lord, planet, kind, rule ids, sources, the outlook's `why`) are
listed as not shown and stay as they were for the chart pages.

## Understanding a question in the person's own words (`understand.rs`)

Kshana is offline; a typed or spoken question is matched to the set of questions the
engine can answer by cue words, in English, Malayalam and Hindi, in their own scripts and
in the Latin letters people type ("kalyanam eppo", "shaadi kab hogi"). The rule is never
to answer a question that was not understood.

1. **Care first**: words of self-harm get a gentle reply with Tele-MANAS (14416) and 112.
2. **Outside a reading**: the day of a death or a length of life, a baby's sex (also
   against the PCPNDT Act), lottery or betting numbers and stock tips, diagnoses and
   medicines, black magic and curses, another person's private acts or feelings, names
   and looks, exact sums, lost things, elections and matches, and someone else's own life
   without their birth details. Each gets a plain "this is outside what Kshana can
   answer" with the reason, and related questions it can answer.
3. **Known questions**: 50 matters × 6 kinds of question; a part of life; the day, week,
   month, year, years ahead; what helps; "is something wrong in my chart"; two people;
   a good day for something; a look back. Single cues and combinations (a relative and a
   health word is that relative's health; a job and a country is a job abroad); specific
   matters win over general ones; ties between matters of different lives are not
   decided: the person picks.
4. **The gate**: an answer is given only when most of the person's meaningful words were
   explained by the matched cues. Otherwise: "not sure it understood, will not guess",
   with the closest questions to pick from.

Measured with three sets of phrasings (tests `every_phrasing…`, `held_out`, `third_set`):
192 core phrasings all right; 139 unseen: 132 right, 7 honestly unsure, 0 wrong; a third
set of 94 written after, first run 54 right / 27 unsure / 13 wrong (several dangerous),
after the gate and general rules 61 right / 33 unsure / 0 wrong. A wrong answer is a
failure; "not sure, pick one" is the safe outcome.

## Calendar

Tithi observances (Ekadashi, Pradosha, Chaturthi, Ashtami, Purnima, Amavasya) carry the
exact instant the tithi begins and the civil day it is observed on: the day whose
sunrise, at the person's place, falls inside the tithi (the udaya rule; convention:
no Vaishnava/Smarta split applied). Lunations are the exact Sun–Moon conjunction or
opposition. Sankranti is the Sun's exact entry into a sidereal sign.

## Jaimini

Chara karakas use the seven-planet scheme (Sun through Saturn, Rahu excluded), ranked by
degree within the sign; the eight-karaka scheme with Rahu is a known alternative and is
not used. Arudha padas follow the standard exception (the pada falling in the house or
its 7th moves to the 10th from there).

## Naming

The 27 × 4 syllable table (namakshara) is the one printed in Indian almanacs; regional
variants exist and the app shows the nakshatra and pada beside the syllable so a family
can consult its own.

## The day's clocks

Ghati readouts under three named conventions (fixed 24 minutes; 30 per day and 30 per
night; 60 per sunrise-to-sunrise). The thirty muhurtas and their qualities follow
Muhurta Chintamani as tabulated; choghadiya day and night sequences follow Drik
Panchang and the JHora tables (the night sequence is its own cycle, not the day's
restarted). Upagraha kalas per BPHS 3.66–67 with the start/middle/end conventions
named and JHora's defaults. Special lagnas per BPHS ch. 3 and 5; Sree lagna = lagna +
360° × the Moon's nakshatra fraction. Gandanta ±3°20′ of the water–fire junctions;
Panchaka from 0° Aquarius to the end of Revati; Bhadra = Vishti karana; nine
inauspicious yogas. The Pancha Pakshi activity matrix is decoded from the JHora
database and could not be verified against a primary text: the engine marks those
functions and the app says so.

The sunrise every clock hangs on follows the chosen convention: Hindu (the Sun's
centre on the true horizon) by default, or Western (the refracted upper limb), set
once in Settings and applied to every ghati, hora, choghadiya and muhurta.

## The lexicon

Every regional name (Malayalam, Tamil, Hindi, Telugu, Kannada) is stored with the
source it was read from; a cell the sources did not confirm is left empty and the
screen shows a dash rather than a guess. Kshana is a Drik panchangam: its tithi and
nakshatra endings can differ from a Vakya almanac by up to a few ghatis, and the
lexicon says so.
