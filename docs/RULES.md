# Rule catalogue

Every sentence Kshana shows a user traces to one of these rule ids. The id is stable;
the source names the text the rule is taken from. "Convention" marks a point where
schools differ and states the choice made.

## Forecast (the Loop)

Nine plain life areas (Love, Career, Money, Health, Family, Study, Home, Travel,
Children) map to the KP/Parashari areas (Marriage, Career, Wealth, Health, Family,
Education, Property, Foreign, Children). A life's score at an instant is the weighted
mean of six components; a day or fortnight sample uses the first four, renormalised.
Every component carries the words that explain it, and the app shows them on demand.

## Ask

A question about one life is always answered. Four sources, each in [0, 1]:

| Source | Weight | What |
|---|---|---|
| natal | 0.35 | KS-N-0001, the birth chart's promise for the life |
| period | 0.25 | 0.6 × (agreeing Vimshottari levels ÷ 5) + 0.4 × the life score now |
| ahead | 0.25 | mean of the next 180 daily samples, half with the best stretch |
| witness | 0.15 | the KP horary verdict for the instant and place of asking (KS-K-0001, KS-P-0012): Yes 1.0, Delayed 0.6, Unclear 0.5, No 0.2 |

Verdicts: Yes ≥ 0.62, Yes with patience ≥ 0.50, Not yet ≥ 0.40, else Not in this
period. Confidence is 1 − 2.5 × the standard deviation of the four sources, clamped
to [0.3, 0.95]. "Is now a good time" is answered from the life score at the instant and
the best remaining hour of the day. The window to act in is the nearest run of at least
two days at or above the verdict's floor; the first opening is the first Moon window
of the horary chart's ruling planets within 30 days (KS-P-0020). The Moon sub-lord's
"genuineness" (KS-P-0001) is reported as a reason, never used to refuse an answer.


## Explain (why now, who, when it eases)

Every component of a life score is opened to its grahas. Each running period lord, each
transiting graha, the Moon's star, the planetary hour and the limbs of the day becomes a
*cause* with a signed pull (its weight × (score − ½), the transits split by graha as
relevance × value × ashtakavarga factor ÷ Σrelevance) and the exact instant it ends: the
period boundary, the graha's next sidereal sign change (solved to the second on the
apparent longitude and the ayanamsa of that instant; the osculating node for Rahu and
Ketu), the next nakshatra boundary, the hour's end, the window's end. For every cause
that ends within two years the component is recomputed with that one graha moved on
(the new five-level chain after a period ends; the new house from the Moon after an
ingress; the next tara; the next hora lord) and the difference is reported as the
*relief*, in points. A monthly *slow* score (birth chart, periods, transits of the eight
grahas other than the Moon, renormalised) over thirty-six months gives the turning
points: the first month the life climbs a band, the peak, the first month it slips.

## Plan (how is this time for me)

A purpose names the lives it touches and their weights (meeting: career 0.30, money
0.20, love 0.20, family 0.15, travel 0.15; interview: career 0.70, money 0.20, study
0.10; exam: study 0.70, career 0.20, health 0.10; journey: travel 0.60, health 0.20,
money 0.10, family 0.10; deal: money 0.50, career 0.30, home 0.20; doctor: health
0.70, family 0.15, money 0.15; date: love 0.70, family 0.15, travel 0.15; family
matter: family 0.60, love 0.20, children 0.20; property: home 0.60, money 0.30, family
0.10; starting something: career 0.30, money 0.30, study, travel, home, health 0.10
each; anything: all nine equally). The reading is the weighted mean of the lives'
scores at the instant (the full forecast, KS-N through KS-M). Inside Rahu kala,
Yamaganda or Gulika it is capped at 0.40 and the verdict is "Shift it if you can";
inside Abhijit it gains 0.05 (Muhurta Chintamani). Verdicts: Go ≥ 0.62, Go with care
≥ 0.50, Shift it ≥ 0.40, else Avoid. Better hours the same day are the planetary hours
outside the cautions whose weighted hora fit beats the chosen hour's by 0.08; a better
day is the best of the next fourteen daily samples when it beats the chosen time by 0.08.

## The briefing

What an astrologer would say across the table, assembled from the rules above and
nothing else: the birth Moon's sign and star (stated first, so any other reading can be
checked against it: a different Moon sign means a birth time off by hours); the
mahadasha and antardasha with their end instants; Saturn's phase relative to the Moon
with its exit, found by walking Saturn's sidereal sign changes ahead and treating a
retrograde step out and back within 1.5 years as a pause, so the date agrees with the
Book; the moment (overall band, strongest and weakest life with their reasons, the
hour, the Moon's star and tara); the days to circle (the best of the next thirty daily
samples for each chosen life); the remedy in brief (the parihara plan for the weakest
chosen life when it is below good, its first three non-optional entries); and, when
that life is steady or lower, its heaviest lasting cause and first lasting lift from
Explain. Under Us the second chart's sentences are turned to the third person, and the
pair gets its own paragraphs: the two seasons, the Moon-to-Moon angle, which side the
wait is on, and the first lasting lift for either. The match's doshas (Nadi, Bhakoot,
Gana, unbalanced Kuja, low Graha Maitri, low Yoni) each carry their classical remedies
(Dharma Sindhu and Nirnaya Sindhu dosha parihara, the Vivaha tradition, Lal Kitab) with
ritual items marked optional.

## The book of a life

Chapters are the mahadashas of the 120-year Vimshottari cycle, pages the antardashas of
the running one; each has a tone per life from the lord's KP signification and
Parashari period quality (a page blends its own lord and the chapter's, half each).
Saturn's trials are every sade sati (Saturn in 12, 1, 2 from the Moon) and dhaiya (4,
8) from birth to age 100 or the kernel's end, from every sidereal sign change of Saturn
solved to the second; a retrograde loop that leaves and re-enters within 1.5 years is one
trial. Jupiter's passages are its sign changes three years back to twelve ahead, each
house marked favourable by the transit table (2, 5, 7, 9, 11). Returns are the instants
Saturn, Jupiter and Rahu stand again at their natal sidereal longitude, from two years
after birth, with crossings under 1.5 years apart counted once.

## Parihara

Remedies are keyed to the graha located as the cause (the weakest running period lord,
or the lord of the area's house). Each graha carries a dictionary of fourteen to
fifteen entries across seven kinds (karma, seva, discipline, daana, vrata, mantra,
pooja), each with its day, effort and source: the dharma reading of the graha's
signification, BPHS Graha shanti (japa counts and deities), the Puranic graha-daana
lists, Lal Kitab, and the Navagraha kshetra tradition. When the life is known, six
more entries for that matter are added (the marriage vratas, Lakshmi for wealth,
Mahamrityunjaya for health, Saraswati for study, and so on). Karma, seva and discipline
rank above daana and vrata; mantra and pooja are marked optional. Conduct items follow the dharma-shastra reading
of each graha's signification; ritual items follow common temple and Lal Kitab practice.
